🗺️ The Journey

Engineering a self-hosted ecosystem, one commit at a time.

How It Started

I stumbled across a website using a widget-based system similar to what you see here. My first thought was to hire someone or find a service to build something like that. Then I looked at the cost. Then I looked closer at the code. And I thought: "This doesn't seem that hard... I could probably figure this out myself."

I'd been in the tech sphere for years - programming robots with MATLAB in high school, modding Minecraft with C++ and Java, jailbreaking phones and messing around in terminal. I knew my way around computers and had coded on and off, but never as a career. Still, web development? That was a whole different beast. HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, APIs, deployment - this was harder than it looked. Way harder. But that's what made it worth it.

What started as "I just want a better viewing experience for my photography" in September 2025 quickly evolved into something bigger. First it was the portfolio. Then I wanted to embed my podcast. Then I needed to learn APIs. Before I knew it, 498 commits later, I'd built an entire website ecosystem: portfolio galleries, blog system with Google Docs integration, admin tools, automated workflows, Cloudflare Workers, and yes - this very roadmap widget you're reading. What began as making a better photography site turned me into a full-stack developer. Or at least, that's the plan when I eventually move off Squarespace.

This roadmap isn't just a project tracker - it's the story of scope creep done right. What started as wanting better photo galleries turned into podcast embedding, then API integration, then becoming a full-stack developer. It's proof that if you put your mind to it and work through the challenges and tribulations, you might get something good out of it. That doesn't mean bad things won't happen - they will. But building something, learning something, pushing through those struggles - it sets you up so they don't hit as hard. When you know you can figure things out, you're ready for whatever comes next.
