Frequently Asked Questions

What's the typical turnaround time?
Preview gallery (sneak peeks): Within 72 hours of service. Final edited gallery: 2-4 weeks depending on project scope and season. Rush delivery available for additional fee.
Do you backup my photos?
Yes. We maintain redundant backups on multiple drives and cloud storage for 90 days after final delivery. After that period, clients are responsible for maintaining their own backups. We recommend downloading and backing up your gallery immediately upon delivery.
What about model releases or property releases?
Required for certain commercial uses—arranged case-by-case. If you plan to use images for advertising, product packaging, or commercial licensing, we'll coordinate proper releases with subjects and property owners. Editorial and personal use typically doesn't require releases.
Can I share my photos on social media?
Yes! Personal social media sharing is encouraged. Please credit @mcc_cal or McCal Media when posting. Commercial use (ads, promotions, business marketing) requires proper licensing—see your project agreement.
What if it rains on my outdoor shoot?
We monitor weather forecasts closely. For outdoor sessions, we'll discuss backup indoor locations or reschedule options if severe weather is expected. Light rain can create beautiful moody photos if you're game! See our Cancellations & Force Majeure sections for policy details.
Do you provide raw/unedited files?
Our standard deliverables are professionally edited high-resolution JPEGs. Raw files are not included unless specifically negotiated and paid for, as they represent our unfinished creative work. Edited files showcase our artistic vision and professional standards.
How many photos will I receive?
Varies by project type. Concert coverage: 50-150 edited images. Corporate events: 100-300 images. Portrait sessions: 25-75 images. Specific deliverable counts are outlined in your project agreement.

Defamation Policy

Effective date: March 1, 2022

Applies to all published or transmitted statements in any medium (site, social platforms, email, print, verbal).

Applies to: Any parties in contact with and associated with McCal Media, whether online or in person. This includes all communications through mcc-cal.com, social media platforms, websites, publications, email, messaging services, in-person conversations, and any other form of public or private communication.

McCal Media takes false statements that harm reputation seriously and maintains a zero-tolerance policy for defamatory content.

What Constitutes Defamation

Defamation includes false statements that:

  • Damage someone's reputation, business, or livelihood
  • Are presented as fact (not opinion or satire)
  • Are published or communicated to third parties
  • Cause demonstrable harm
  • Include libel (written) or slander (spoken)

Prohibited Content

We prohibit:

  • False statements about individuals or businesses
  • Fabricated reviews or testimonials
  • Misleading claims about products or services
  • False accusations of criminal behavior
  • Knowingly spreading false information
  • Impersonation or misrepresentation

Reporting Defamatory Content

If you believe content on our site is defamatory:

  • Contact us immediately at contact@mcc-cal.com
  • Include: URL/location of content, explanation of false statements, evidence of harm
  • Provide documentation supporting your claim
  • We will review and respond within 48 hours

Our Response

Upon receiving a valid complaint, we will:

  • Investigate the claim thoroughly and promptly
  • Remove or correct content found to be defamatory
  • Take action against users who post defamatory content
  • Cooperate with legal authorities when necessary
  • Preserve evidence for potential legal proceedings

Consequences

Users who post defamatory content may face:

  • Immediate content removal
  • Account suspension or permanent ban
  • Legal action and liability for damages
  • Reporting to law enforcement if applicable

Legal Disclaimer

This policy does not constitute legal advice. If you believe you have been defamed, consult with an attorney regarding your legal rights and remedies.
