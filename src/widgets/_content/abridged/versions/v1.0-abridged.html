What makes Abridged different

Classic Mode

Typography-first reading with clean spacing, zero ads, and a clear “done for today” state.

Abridged Mode (RSVP)

Rapid Serial Visual Presentation with adjustable WPM, smart pauses, and haptics for confidence.

Community-first

Curated Pittsburgh stories, events, and local resources—no engagement gimmicks or algorithmic feeds.

Emotional safety

Grounding mode, consent walls for sensitive content, and calming alternatives when you need a break.

Roadmap

  • Phase 1 · MVP Navigation, design system, mocked content, saved articles.
  • Phase 2 · Dynamic RSS ingest, ArticleService, pull-to-refresh, optimized local storage.
  • Phase 3 · Abridged Mode RSVP player, tokenizer, WPM controls, smart pauses.
  • Phase 4 · Offline Full caching, dark mode, settings polish, onboarding.
