Caleb McCartney is a Pittsburgh‑based photojournalist and freelance storyteller, specializing in news-driven narratives, high-energy concert coverage, and authentic brand visuals. A proud alumnus of Point Park University's BFA program, Caleb has spent the last 6+ years documenting the pulse of the city—bridging the gap between professional photojournalism and commercial excellence.

His approach is rooted in the "decisive moment," a philosophy carried from his roots in news photography into the world of corporate events and live performance. Whether he is on the front lines of a breaking story or the front row of a sold-out arena, Caleb’s work is driven by a commitment to authentic connection and unscripted truth.

In 2026, he continues to lead as a premier choice for Photojournalism, Concert, and Event photography in Pittsburgh and beyond, collaborating with names like the New York Post and Pittsburgh Magazine to tell the stories that define our era.

When he’s not behind the lens, Caleb hosts the "Caffeinated Connections" podcast, where he talks with industry professionals about the intersection of creativity and impact.
