Last night, or maybe this morning, I had a dream. Or more accurately, I was inside some kind of conversation with myself, my other self, and my subconscious, or at least whatever is permissible by those terms. I am still trying to understand what it was.

All I know is that I was sitting in an old sandbox from a school in California that I knew when I was younger. A kid beside me was shoveling sand with a tiny plastic shovel that was barely doing anything. Then he said to me, "You know that nobody likes you, right?" He kept filling his pail while every word landed with its own weight.

I honestly did not know what to say. I did not know him, but he looked familiar. I cannot remember his face, only what he said and the way he said it. It sounded simple, like common knowledge, but there was something snide in it, something meant to injure.

He went on to tell me that nobody would love me. It was a putdown, but the strange part was that I stayed there with him. I did not get up. I did not walk away. I kept sitting with him, ignoring it as if he did not mean it, or as if I was only hearing things.

Around the sandbox there was a gate. It never occurred to me that the real sandbox had not looked like that; in the dream the gate was closer, more immediate. While I sat there questioning why I was still with him, a woman passed by the gate and told me, "I am your friend no matter what, and don't listen to him. He is just trying to question your self-judgment. Don't let it get to you." Then she vanished.

She was younger, with black hair and a white handkerchief tied over it, patterned in a way I can almost remember. That is about all I have left of her. But what she said stayed with me after I woke up.

They were complete sentences. They made some sense. Was I telling myself these things because my own mind was making them up? I can only say that it felt like part of a broken conversation with myself.
