As someone who has lived through the trauma of sexual coercion, and through having "no" treated as if it were never said at all, this piece sits uncomfortably close to me. The body remembers what other people dismiss. Trauma can leave marks that are not always visible, but that does not make them any less real. These bruises do not fade easily. Memory serves us at its worst when it keeps returning with the same ruthless force.

```
Fingers on my body
imaginary as they seem
Before the thoughts escape me
I only try to scream

I wake up in my room
afraid of who I am
Do I know if it's only
just inside my head

Nightmares when I'm waking
walking every day
Suffering, dying, longing
hoping that I stay

There is this slight feeling
that bites at me at night
As if they are drawing closer
I just want to lose my sight

I close my eyes so tightly
it's only in my head
Why won't it go away?
It stands there, mocking instead

To live a life so easy
simple as can be
As if this never happened
but it keeps haunting me

Alas, these scars tell otherwise
deep within my wounds
Unseen by any others
yet forever they consume
```
