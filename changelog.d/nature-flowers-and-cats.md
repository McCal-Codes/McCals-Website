### Flowers, Pollinators, and Two Cats Join Nature

The Nature page has two new collections. Flowers and Pollinators gathers 26 frames of zinnias, coneflowers, black-eyed Susans, butterflies and a bee, mostly from Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens in Pittsburgh. Sinbad and Salem shows nine portraits of the two cats on a bookshelf. Every frame carries a written caption.
